## 1. Executive Legal Overview

This research document defines the statutory foundations and exact mathematical rules governing investment income received by individual resident investors in Brazil across calendar years 2025 and 2026.

Unlike capital gains (which are subject to monthly trade matching, net profit determination, exempt volume limits, and loss carry-forward under Lei 8.981/95 and Lei 11.033/04), **investment proceeds (proventos)** represent cash distributions governed by dedicated withholding at source and statutory exemption regimes.

## 2. Statutory Legal Matrix by Income Type

### 2.1. Dividends Distributed by Brazilian Legal Entities

#### Calendar Year 2025:
- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 9.249/1995, Art. 10; RIR/2018 (Decreto nº 9.580/2018), Art. 698.
- **Tax Treatment:** `ISENTO_NAO_TRIBUTAVEL` (`EXEMPT`).
- **Withholding at Source:** 0% (`0.00`).
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-DIV-2025` (Version: `1995-12-26-LEI-9249`).
- **Conditions:** Distributed out of profits generated by Brazilian corporate entities.

#### Calendar Year 2026:
- **Statutory Source:** Legislação do IRPF 2026 / Nova Redação do Art. 10 da Lei nº 9.249/1995 (conforme Emenda Constitucional nº 132/2023 e Lei Complementar / Ordinária de Reforma da Renda).
- **Tax Treatment:**
  - **Within Monthly Exemption Threshold (<= R$ 50.000,00 per paying source per calendar month):** `ISENTO_NAO_TRIBUTAVEL` (`EXEMPT`). Withholding = 0%.
  - **Exceeding Monthly Exemption Threshold (> R$ 50.000,00 per paying source per calendar month):** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`). Withholding = 10% on the excess amount over R$ 50.000,00.
- **Aggregation Scope:** Strictly scoped to the **same paying source** (same CNPJ / payerId), **same beneficiary** (individual taxpayer), within the **same calendar month** (`competence`, e.g., `2026-04`).
- **Payer Isolation:** Dividends from distinct paying sources are evaluated independently; thresholds do not cross payer boundaries.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-DIV-2026` (Version: `2025-12-30-LEI-DIV-2026`).

### 2.2. Juros sobre Capital Próprio (JCP)

#### Calendar Year 2025:
- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 9.249/1995, Art. 9º, § 2º; Lei nº 14.789/2023.
- **Withholding Rate:** 15.0% (`0.15`).
- **Tax Treatment:** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`).
- **Definitive Nature:** The withholding is final and definitive. It cannot be used as a credit to offset monthly 6015 DARF obligations on capital gains.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-JCP-2025` (Version: `1995-12-26-LEI-9249`).

#### Calendar Year 2026:
- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 14.789/2023 c/c Legislação Tributária 2026 (Alíquota atualizada de JCP).
- **Withholding Rate:** 17.5% (`0.175`).
- **Tax Treatment:** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`).
- **Definitive Nature:** Retenção exclusiva na fonte definitiva; zero compensação contra ganho de capital.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-JCP-2026` (Version: `2025-12-30-LEI-JCP-2026`).

### 2.3. Rendimentos de Fundos de Investimento Imobiliário (FII)

#### Statutory Regime (2025 & 2026):
- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 11.033/2004, Art. 3º, Inciso III, com redação dada pela Lei nº 14.754/2023 (vigente desde 01/01/2024).
- **Cumulative Exemption Criteria (Art. 3º, III):**
  1. **Trading Platform:** Quotas must be admitted to trading exclusively on organized exchange or organized OTC market (`isListed === true`).
  2. **Investor Dispersion:** The fund must possess at least 100 quota-holders (`investorCountAtLeast100 === true`).
  3. **Ownership Limitation:** The individual investor (together with related parties) must not hold 10% or more of total fund quotas or rights (`beneficiaryOwnershipLessThan10Percent === true`).
- **Outcomes:**
  - **All 3 conditions verified (or explicit `isVerified === true`):** `ISENTO_NAO_TRIBUTAVEL` (`EXEMPT`), Withholding = 0%. Status = `COMPLETE`. Rule: `R-FII-EXEMPT` (Version: `2023-12-29-LEI-14754`).
  - **Any condition explicitly failed:** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`, 20% IRRF at source under Lei nº 9.779/1999, Art. 2º). Status = `COMPLETE`. Rule: `R-FII-TAXABLE` (Version: `2023-12-29-LEI-14754`).
  - **Eligibility unverified / missing facts:** Fails closed to `REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION` (`FII_ELIGIBILITY_UNVERIFIED`). Status = `REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`. Rule: `R-FII-UNVERIFIED` (Version: `2023-12-29-LEI-14754`).

## 3. Structural Invariants & Boundaries

1. **No Capital-Gains Contamination:**
   Investment proceeds never adjust average cost basis (PM) of open positions, nor do they reduce accumulated variable-income tax losses.
2. **Deterministic Replay:**
   Income reporting is purely derived on demand from ledger events (`DIVIDEND`, `JCP`, `FII_INCOME`).
3. **Payer Canonical Identity:**
   Every income event is tied to an authoritative paying source identified by `AssetId` and official issuer metadata (CNPJ/ticker). Free-text matching is prohibited.
4. **Zero Float IEEE 754 Math:**
   All gross, withholding, and net figures use exact arbitrary-precision decimal strings (`Money` encapsulation).
5. **No Global Minimum Tax Engine:**
   Phase 4H.4/4H.4A captures and categorizes income facts authoritatively; high-income global minimum tax calculation is out of scope.
