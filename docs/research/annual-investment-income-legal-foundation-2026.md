## 1. Executive Legal Overview

This research document defines the statutory foundations and exact mathematical rules governing investment income received by individual resident investors in Brazil across calendar years 2025 and 2026.

Unlike capital gains (which are subject to monthly trade matching, net profit determination, exempt volume limits, and loss carry-forward under Lei 8.981/95 and Lei 11.033/04), **investment proceeds (proventos)** represent cash distributions governed by dedicated withholding at source and statutory exemption regimes.

## 2. Statutory Legal Matrix by Income Type

### 2.1. Dividends Distributed by Brazilian Legal Entities

#### Calendar Year 2025:

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 9.249/1995, Art. 10; RIR/2018 (Decreto nº 9.580/2018), Art. 698.
- **Tax Treatment:** `ISENTO_NAO_TRIBUTAVEL` (`EXEMPT`).
- **Withholding at Source:** 0% (`0.00`).
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-DIV-2025` (Version: `1995-12-26-LEI-9249`).
- **Conditions:** Distributed out of profits generated by Brazilian corporate entities.

#### Calendar Year 2026+ (Ordinary Rule):

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 15.270/2025, Art. 2º (inclui Art. 6º-A na Lei nº 9.250/1995).
- **Effective Date:** 1º de janeiro de 2026.
- **Tax Treatment:**
  - **Monthly total ≤ R$ 50.000,00 per paying source per calendar month:** `ISENTO_NAO_TRIBUTAVEL` (`EXEMPT`). Withholding = 0%.
  - **Monthly total > R$ 50.000,00 ("superior a"):** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`). Withholding = **10% on the TOTAL monthly distributed amount** (NOT on the excess).
- **Withholding Base:** "sobre o total do valor pago, creditado, empregado ou entregue" (Art. 6º-A, _caput_).
- **No Deductions:** "São vedadas quaisquer deduções da base de cálculo" (Art. 6º-A, § 1º).
- **Recalculation:** If multiple payments occur in the same month by the same source to the same beneficiary, the tax must be recalculated on the accumulated total (Art. 6º-A, § 2º).
- **Aggregation Scope:** Strictly scoped to the **same paying source** (same CNPJ / payerId), **same beneficiary** (individual taxpayer), within the **same calendar month**.
- **Payer Isolation:** Dividends from distinct paying sources are evaluated independently; thresholds do not cross payer boundaries.
- **"Efeito Degrau" (Cliff Effect):** R$ 50.000,00 → IRRF = R$ 0. R$ 50.000,01 → IRRF = R$ 5.000,001 (10% of total).
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-DIV-2026` (Version: `2025-LEI-15270`).

#### Transitional Treatment (Pre-2026 Profits):

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 15.270/2025, Art. 6º-A, § 3º da Lei nº 9.250/1995.
- **Legal Text (§ 3º):**
  > "§ 3º Não se sujeitam à retenção na fonte de que trata este artigo os lucros e dividendos:
  > I - relativos a resultados apurados até o ano-calendário de 2025;
  > II - cuja distribuição tenha sido aprovada até 31 de dezembro de 2025; e
  > III - exigíveis nos termos da legislação civil ou empresarial, desde que o pagamento, crédito, emprego ou entrega ocorra nos termos originalmente previstos no ato de aprovação."
- **Cumulative Requirements:**
  1. Profits relate to results accrued through year-end 2025;
  2. Distribution formally approved by the corporate body by 31/12/2025;
  3. Payment occurs in the terms originally defined in the approval act (covering 2026, 2027, or 2028).
- **Missing Evidence:** Fails closed to ordinary R-DIV-2026 treatment.
- **No Free-Text Inference:** Transition eligibility requires explicit structured evidence.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-DIV-TRANSITION` (Version: `2025-LEI-15270-TRANSITION`).

### 2.2. Juros sobre Capital Próprio (JCP)

#### Calendar Year 2025:

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 9.249/1995, Art. 9º, § 2º; Lei nº 14.789/2023.
- **Withholding Rate:** 15.0% (`0.15`).
- **Tax Treatment:** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`).
- **Definitive Nature:** The withholding is final and definitive.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-JCP-2025` (Version: `1995-12-26-LEI-9249`).

#### Calendar Year 2026:

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 14.789/2023 c/c Legislação Tributária 2026.
- **Withholding Rate:** 17.5% (`0.175`).
- **Tax Treatment:** `TRIBUTACAO_EXCLUSIVA_FONTE` (`WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE`).
- **Definitive Nature:** Retenção exclusiva na fonte definitiva; zero compensação contra ganho de capital.
- **Rule Identifier:** `R-JCP-2026` (Version: `2025-12-30-LEI-JCP-2026`).

### 2.3. Rendimentos de Fundos de Investimento Imobiliário (FII)

#### Statutory Regime (2025 & 2026):

- **Statutory Source:** Lei nº 11.033/2004, Art. 3º, Inciso III, com redação dada pela Lei nº 14.754/2023.
- **Cumulative Exemption Criteria (Art. 3º, III):**
  1. **Trading Platform:** Quotas must be admitted to trading exclusively on organized exchange (`isListed === true`).
  2. **Investor Dispersion:** The fund must possess at least 100 quota-holders (`investorCountAtLeast100 === true`).
  3. **Ownership Limitation:** The individual investor must not hold 10% or more of total fund quotas (`beneficiaryOwnershipLessThan10Percent === true`).
- **Outcomes:**
  - **All 3 conditions verified (or explicit `isVerified === true`):** `EXEMPT`, Withholding = 0%. Rule: `R-FII-EXEMPT`.
  - **Any condition explicitly failed:** `WITHHOLDING_EXCLUSIVE` (20% IRRF). Rule: `R-FII-TAXABLE`.
  - **Eligibility unverified / missing facts:** Fails closed to `REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION`. Rule: `R-FII-UNVERIFIED`.

## 4. Structural Invariants & Boundaries

1. **No Capital-Gains Contamination:** Investment proceeds never adjust average cost basis or reduce tax losses.
2. **Deterministic Replay:** Income reporting is purely derived on demand from ledger events.
3. **Payer Canonical Identity:** Every income event is tied to an authoritative paying source identified by `AssetId`.
4. **Zero Float IEEE 754 Math:** All figures use exact arbitrary-precision decimal strings (`Money`).
5. **Total-Base Withholding:** 2026+ dividend withholding is on the TOTAL monthly amount, not the excess.
6. **No Global Minimum Tax Engine:** Phase 4H.4 captures facts only; high-income minimum tax is out of scope.
7. **Transition Fail-Closed:** Unconfirmed transition claims fail closed to `REQUIRES_CONFIRMATION` (`R-DIV-TRANSITION-UNVERIFIED`) with null withholding and `TRANSITION_ELIGIBILITY_UNCONFIRMED` blocking reason; ordinary dividends without transition claim or with explicit ineligibility evaluate under `R-DIV-2026`.
